Van't Hoff Plot Generator

Enter temperature in °C and Ksp values. The program converts temperature to Kelvin, calculates 1/T and ln(Ksp), performs linear regression, and generates the van't Hoff plot.

Van't Hoff equation: ln(Ksp) = −ΔH°/R × (1/T) + ΔS°/R. Therefore ΔH° = −mR and ΔS° = bR, using R = 8.314462618 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹. The units of ΔH° are J/mol and ΔS° are J/(mol·K).
